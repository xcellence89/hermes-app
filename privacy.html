Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What Hermes is

Hermes is a private personal assistant used by one person, its owner, to manage his own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, Hermes accesses only the Google services the owner approves on the consent screen (mainly Google Calendar). It reads and changes the owner's own events when he asks it to.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to carry out the owner's requests.
  • The sign-in token is stored on the owner's private server and is not shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops Hermes from reaching the account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
